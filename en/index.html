Procedures

What we treat

Complex cases that general dentistry and conventional implantology refer out.

Severe bone loss

Zygomatic implants

"You don't have enough bone for implants" is not the final word.

When the upper jaw has lost too much bone for conventional implants, zygomatic implants anchor in the cheekbone, which never resorbs. Fixed teeth without months of bone grafting — often with immediate loading.

High-complexity surgery that requires CT imaging, virtual planning and a team that performs it regularly.

Rehabilitation

Full-mouth implants & bone reconstruction

Rebuild the foundation before replacing the tooth.

Implants where surgery goes beyond placement: grafting, sinus lifts, guided bone regeneration, or full rehabilitation of an entire arch — planned digitally from the start.

Facial skeleton

Orthognathic (jaw) surgery

When the problem is the bone, not the teeth.

Corrects the position of the upper and lower jaws — for bite problems, facial asymmetry and skeletal sleep apnea. Planned virtually so the skeletal result is simulated before you decide.

Joint

Total TMJ replacement

When the jaw joint is destroyed, it can be replaced.

For end-stage arthritis, ankylosis or failed previous surgeries: the joint is replaced with a patient-specific prosthesis designed on your own CT scan. A major, rarely performed operation — and the only real solution when there is no joint left to preserve.

Process

How it works

Nothing is decided before your imaging and your written plan exist.

1 · Remote evaluation

Send your CT scan and dental history by email or WhatsApp. You receive an honest assessment — surgical or not, options, timelines — before booking any travel.

2 · Written plan & quote

A written treatment plan with alternatives, real timelines and a detailed cost estimate. You decide with complete information.

3 · Your trip to Santiago

Surgery is scheduled around your travel. Most implant cases require a stay of about one to two weeks; complex jaw surgery may require longer. Recovery happens in a modern, walkable city.

4 · Surgery & follow-up

With follow-up defined before you travel, and a written report for your dentist back home so your long-term care continues where you live.

Your surgeon

He is staff surgeon at the Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery Service of the Military Hospital of Santiago — the hospital volume that builds skill in trauma and complex cases.

He works with Cone Beam CT and virtual surgical planning in every complex case: implant positions and osteotomy lines are resolved on the computer before surgery, and arrive in the operating room as patient-specific surgical guides.
